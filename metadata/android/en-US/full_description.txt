PepsGoal helps you stick to your goals, one day at a time.

🔥 Streaks and flames
Check off your goals every day and watch your streak grow. Streak freezes, rest days and vacations: one slip doesn't ruin everything.

🎯 Every kind of goal
Daily habits, numeric targets, durations, limits not to exceed, steps, chains… and a mini version of a habit for tough days.

📊 Clear statistics
Week, month, year in pixels, mood correlations, recaps you can share.

🤝 With your friends
Duos, weekly leagues, community challenges and end-to-end encrypted messaging. Share a goal by link or QR code.

📱 Built for Android
Widgets (today, my week…), interactive morning notification, Live Updates, Quick Settings tiles, Google Assistant, NFC tag check-in, Wear OS, Health Connect, tablets and foldables.

🔒 Your privacy first
No-account mode: your data stays on your device. No ads. Free software (MIT license).
