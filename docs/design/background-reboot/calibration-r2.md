# Correction après critique indépendante — calibration r2

Les masters v1 Kopa et Loire ont été réellement inspectés par designer et critique. Perspective et ancrage géographique sont beaucoup plus convaincants que les brouillons précédents. Ils sont refusés comme référence finale de collection : traitement trop semi-réaliste et micro-détaillé; Loire comporte des halos peints dans le ciel transparent.

## Kopa — édition du master v1

Keep the exact stadium viewpoint, diagonal football pitch, compact rectangular covered stands, black-and-white SCO supporter identity, roof framing and generous quiet sky of the supplied illustration. RESTYLE substantially into clean, bold, premium CARTOON editorial environment art consistent with a playful colorful mobile application. Use large intentional rounded shapes and clear flat color planes with only two or three shading values per material. Reduce tiny details by at least70percent: group distant spectators into simple irregular color masses, simplify foreground hair and clothing into chunky friendly silhouettes, remove individual hair strands and realistic facial anatomy, remove grass blades and photo-like roof/grain texture. Keep beautiful depth and strong lighting through crisp warm cream highlight planes and cool plum #2A1420 or blue-violet shadow planes; clear contact shadows, restrained edge highlights. Green field vibrant and matte with broad mowing bands. Architecture retains convincing structural thickness and perspective, but no photorealism, no fine painterly realism, no sketch noise, no smooth plastic3Dtoy rendering. Simplify sunset to large calm color bands. No text, logo, watermark or UI. Full opaque composition1024x1536.

## Loire — édition du master v1

Keep the supplied composition and GEOGRAPHY: Saumur castle high on its raised promontory, cream riverside town below, broad Loire, dark wooden gabare and willow on the near bank. RESTYLE substantially as bold clean CARTOON environment illustration, playful premium mobile-app art. Reduce microdetails by at least70percent. Foliage consists of large scalloped clusters with two or three clear color/shadow planes, NOT thousands of leaves. The willow has a few designed graceful drooping leaf masses. Grass and flowers are a few large intentional stylized tufts. Tree bark has four or five broad carved shape marks, not photographic furrows. Water consists of broad blue-green flowing shapes and a few grouped clean reflections, not grain or hundreds of ripples. Castle and houses keep recognizable silhouettes but simplify windows into sparse intentional groups. Warm cream lit faces and clean cool plum-blue shadow faces create volume; rich controlled color, no muddy gray. CRITICAL TRANSPARENCY: outside the exact silhouettes of tree, foliage, castle, houses and distant land, the entire upper sky is true alpha0, completely empty. REMOVE ALL yellow-green glow around tree leaves and ALL gray/green/cream haze around castle or horizon; no colored atmosphere may bleed into transparent sky. Edge antialiasing only one or two pixels. Painted solid surfaces should be fully opaque alpha255. Do not paint sun, moon, clouds or sky. No text, logos, watermarks, UI or checkerboard backdrop.
