# Rooftops — simplification cartoon v2

Masterv1 réellement inspecté par designer après demande du critique. La profondeur entre les mansardes proches, la rue en contrebas et la ville au bord de l'eau est très réussie. Le détail du lierre, des ferronneries, des rangs d'ardoises, des moulures et des façades lointaines est trop miniaturisé et semi-réaliste pour le niveau de simplification validé sur Kopa2/Loire2.

## Prompt d'édition ciblée

Edit the supplied Rooftops v1 artwork to match the bold clean CARTOON style of the Kopa2 and Loire2 calibration. Preserve the EXACT strong composition and architectural perspective: large blue-slate mansard roofs and rounded dormerwindows close on the left, cream façades and terrace, narrow street dropping between buildings, layered roofs receding to a river and bridge in the distance, rightedge building framing. Keep the current transparent upper sky. Do NOT flatten or remove any depthplane. Substantially simplify75percent of microdetail into large intentional shapes and two or three colorvalues per material. ROOFS: broad blue-slate planes, only a few large selective seam marks and broad cool shadowfaces, no dense tiles. ARCHITECTURE: large cream lit planes versus clean cool plum-blue shadowplanes; simplify molding and stonecourses to a few bold steps; distant façades become grouped chunky masses with sparse windowshapes instead of miniature architectural models. BALCONIES: five to seven simple vertical bars with a thick toprail, NO delicate ironfiligree or scrollwork. LEAVES/IVY: a few large scalloped masses or broad leafshapes, not hundreds of individual realistic leaves; flowers as grouped rounded masses. GLASS: large flat dark blue-green windowshapes with one restrained highlightplane, no realistic complex reflections. TERRACECHAIRS/TABLE: simple graphic silhouettes, keep convincing groundcontact and castshadows. Retain warmth/coolness separation and beautiful volume through clear lit/shadowfaces rather than textures. All essential distant city and riveridentity remains within x22to78percent for tall9:19.5crop; decorative edge rooftops may crop as deliberate framing. No photorealism, no semi-realistic filmstill, no glossyplastictoy3D, no grain. Actual transparent sky, no painted sun/moon/permanentclouds, no coloredhaze leaking into emptysky. No text logos watermark UI or checkerboard.
