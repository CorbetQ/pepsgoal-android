# Tram — correction de style et de cadrage v2

Le masterv1 a été réellement inspecté par designer et critique. La livrée blanche/multicolore, l'épaisseur du tram et les rails en perspective sont réussis. Le détail des moulures, garde-corps, pavés, feuillages et reflets est trop réaliste par rapport à Kopa2/Loire2. Le nez du tram est trop près du bord droit pour le recadrage téléphone long.

## Prompt d'édition

Edit the supplied Angers tram illustration. PRESERVE the real Angers tram identity: cream-white rounded body, rainbow-colored window and lowerbody panels, dark blue-green windshield, an articulated train visibly resting on rails that converge into the distance. Keep the quiet transparent upper sky, warm/cool light separation, layered citydepth and inviting street scene. SUBSTANTIALLY RESTYLE into the same BOLD CLEAN CARTOON collection as Kopa2 and Loire2: large intentional rounded masses, two or three distinct color values per material, crisp cream lit faces and cool plum-blue shadowfaces. Remove at least75percent of micro-detail. Architecture: simplify façades to a few chunky wallplanes, large grouped windowshapes and roofsilhouettes; replace ornate sculpture, tiny moldings, iron balcony filigree and tiled stonecourses with sparse clear geometric accents. Tram: simplify realistic glass reflections to two or three flat blue-green windowplanes and a single broad highlight; keep rainbow bands vivid and matte. Pavement: large broad slab shapes with only a few perspective seams, no thousands of tiny cobbles, no photorealistic pebbles or tactilepaving dots. Trees: grouped scalloped leafmasses, not tiny individually painted leaves. Preserve believable volume, contacts with ground, cast shadows and structural perspective through these largecolorplanes. MOBILECROP: reposition or scale the tram so its whole distinctive nose, headlights and windshield are contained inside x40to75percent of masterwidth; its identity cannot depend on x85percent. Important midground landmarks also within x22to78percent; decorative edgearchitecture may crop. Keep enough lowerdepth for converging rails. No new landmarks or extra buildings. Nophotorealism, no realistic filmstill, no grain, no glossy3Dtoy plastic, no text logos labels watermark or UI. Portrait1024x1536 with genuinely transparent upper sky; no painted sun moon permanentclouds or checkerboard.
